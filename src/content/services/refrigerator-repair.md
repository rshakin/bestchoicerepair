Refrigerators are the appliance you can least afford to be without, which is why we prioritize same-day and next-day refrigerator calls. We service standard, French-door, side-by-side, and built-in refrigerators from Samsung, GE, Whirlpool, Maytag, Bosch, Wolf, and Thermador.

When a [refrigerator stops cooling](/problems/refrigerator-not-cooling/) but the freezer still works, the usual causes are a failed evaporator fan motor, a frost-blocked air damper, or a defrost system that's stopped cycling and iced over the coils. When both compartments are warm and the compressor runs non-stop, it points to a bad start relay, a stuck condenser fan, or a sealed-system refrigerant leak. Our technicians test each on-site rather than swapping parts on a guess.

Two local factors shorten refrigerator life in the valley. Fine desert dust packs the condenser coils and forces the compressor to work harder — a coil cleaning once or twice a year genuinely extends the unit's life. And the second refrigerator or freezer that so many Vegas homes keep in the garage runs against 115°F-plus summer heat, which is why garage units fail years ahead of the one in the kitchen.

[Ice makers and water dispensers](/problems/ice-maker-not-working/) are the other frequent call, and in Las Vegas the culprit is often hard-water scale in the inlet valve or fill tube rather than the ice maker itself. We also read [fault codes](/error-codes/refrigerator/) off digital models on-site, and confirm parts availability up front on premium built-in columns.

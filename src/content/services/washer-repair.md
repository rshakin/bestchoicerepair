Whether it's a top-load, front-load, or stackable unit — and whether you call it a washer or a washing machine — our technicians repair every major brand: Samsung, GE, Whirlpool, Maytag, Bosch, LG, and more. Most washer problems (drainage failures, worn belts, bad pumps, faulty lid switches, electronic control issues) can be diagnosed and fixed in a single visit.

A washer that [won't drain](/problems/washer-wont-drain/) or [won't spin](/problems/washer-wont-spin/) is the most common call we get. On top-loaders it's usually a failed lid switch, a worn drive belt, or a stripped motor coupler; on front-loaders it's more often a clogged drain pump, a jammed coin trap, or worn spin bearings that make the tub grind and eventually leak. We diagnose which before quoting parts, so you're not paying for a pump when the problem is a $15 switch.

Las Vegas hard water is hard on washers. Scale builds up in the inlet valves until they no longer shut fully, which leaves a machine slowly filling when it's off or [leaking from the back](/problems/washer-leaking/). Mineral-laden water also shortens the life of the fill hoses and dispenser solenoids. If your washer is over five years old and has never had its hoses replaced, it's worth doing before one bursts.

Digital models add another layer: an [error code on the display](/error-codes/washer/) points to a specific subsystem — drain, fill, door lock, motor — and our technicians read those codes on-site instead of guessing. We carry common washer parts on the truck, so most repairs are completed same-day, backed by our 90-day warranty on parts and labor with the $89 service call fee waived when you move forward with the repair.

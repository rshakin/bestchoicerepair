Enterprise, in the southwest valley along the 215 Beltway, has grown rapidly over the past decade with master-planned communities like Mountain's Edge and Southern Highlands. Most homes here came with newer appliance packages, so the calls we get lean toward wear-and-tear — ice makers, drain pumps, door seals, control boards — rather than the end-of-life failures we see in the valley's older neighborhoods.

Newer appliances don't get a pass on Las Vegas hard water, though. The valley's tap water is among the hardest in the country, and within a few years scale collects on dishwasher spray arms and heating elements, in washing machine inlet valves, and along refrigerator water lines and ice-maker fill tubes. Some Enterprise builders roughed in a water-softener loop in the garage; plenty of homes never had one installed, and those are the ones where a three-year-old dishwasher already isn't drying properly.

The three-car garages standard across Mountain's Edge and Southern Highlands are also where a lot of households keep a second refrigerator or a stand-up freezer. Garage temperatures here run past 115°F in mid-summer, which keeps that compressor running almost continuously and shortens its life by years compared with the unit in the kitchen. A garage fridge that quits during a July heat wave is one of the most common calls we take from this part of the valley.

Our technicians cover all of Enterprise with same-day and next-day appointments, and we're set up for the gated-community and callbox access most of the area's developments require — give us the gate code when you book and there's no delay at the entrance.

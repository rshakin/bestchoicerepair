Summerlin's master-planned villages on the west edge of the valley lean heavily premium — built-in refrigeration columns, professional-style ranges, panel-ready dishwashers, steam and speed ovens from Wolf, Thermador, Sub-Zero, and Bosch. Our technicians work on these platforms regularly and take the extra care a custom-integrated install calls for.

Summerlin sits higher than the valley floor and right up against Red Rock, and that has a real effect on appliances: fine desert dust plus ongoing construction on the community's western edge loads condenser coils and dryer vents faster than in more sheltered parts of town. Sub-Zero condensers in particular need regular coil cleaning here — a unit that's short-cycling or running warm is very often just choked with dust.

Most homes in The Ridges and Redpoint have a whole-home softener, but it's not a complete shield. The line to the refrigerator or a pot-filler is sometimes left un-softened, and softeners that run out of salt quietly stop working — so we still see scale on ice makers, steam-oven reservoirs, and built-in coffee systems. Second Sub-Zeros and wine units kept in the garage or butler's pantry get the added strain of summer heat on top.

We handle standard washer, dryer, and dishwasher repairs across Summerlin's villages too, with same-day appointments available. For the premium built-ins, we check parts lead times up front — some Sub-Zero and Wolf components ship from the manufacturer — so you have a realistic timeline from the first visit.

Magic Chef is a value brand built around compact and apartment-sized appliances, and around the Las Vegas valley you'll find them in condos, casitas, guest houses, garage bars, and short-term rentals. Repairs are usually straightforward, affordable fixes rather than major component work, and parts are widely available, so most calls are done in a single visit.

The most common Magic Chef call is a [compact or mini refrigerator that won't stay cold](/problems/refrigerator-not-cooling/). On these small units it's typically a failed thermostat, a stuck evaporator fan, or a door gasket that's lost its seal and is letting warm air in — all inexpensive parts. Because so many Vegas Magic Chef fridges live in a garage or an outdoor bar area, summer heat pushes the little compressors hard, and a unit that was fine in spring often struggles by July.

Magic Chef ranges are usually gas, and a [burner or oven igniter that won't light](/problems/oven-burner-wont-light/) is the usual complaint — a quick igniter swap in most cases. Their compact dishwashers most often [won't drain fully](/problems/dishwasher-not-draining/) because of a clogged pump or filter, and the stacked washer/dryer combo units common in small Vegas floor plans tend to throw error codes tied to drainage or a stuck door lock.

We stock the common Magic Chef thermostats, igniters, fan motors, and door parts on the truck, so most compact refrigerator, range, and dishwasher repairs finish in one visit.
